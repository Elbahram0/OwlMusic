## Overview

Owl Music is an open-source Android application designed for seamless music discovery, streaming, and local audio management without ads or subscriptions.

### Key Features

- **Online Streaming:** Fast search and high-bitrate playback powered by InnerTube audio extractors.
- **Offline Storage:** Download tracks locally with full metadata and high-resolution cover artwork.
- **Playback Control:** Background service with lockscreen integration, notification controls, and sleep timer.
- **Playlist Management:** Custom playlists and drag-and-drop queue reordering.
- **User Interface:** Modern Material 3 design with pure dark AMOLED and light themes.
- **Localization:** Full multi-language support (English, Turkish, Russian, Chinese).

---

## Tech Stack

- **Language:** Kotlin
- **UI Toolkit:** Jetpack Compose & Material 3
- **Media Engine:** AndroidX Media3 (ExoPlayer & MediaSession)
- **Networking:** OkHttp3
- **Image Loading:** Coil
- **Architecture:** MVVM with Kotlin Coroutines & StateFlow
